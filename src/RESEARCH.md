# Opportunity research — 24 September 2026

The catalog contains 48 distinct starting points: specific programs, participation projects, practice resources, and clearly labeled discovery directories. It is a curated research snapshot, not an exhaustive database or a live application feed. The directories extend discovery beyond the individually reviewed records.

Research used organizer, university, government, and project documentation. We paraphrase factual descriptions and link to the primary source. The outputs and three-step checklists are original MyGapMentor suggestions, not organizer promises. No program applications, accounts, messages, or registrations were submitted during research.

## Findings that affect the product

- NASA OSTEM’s current enrollment requirements supersede older general high-school descriptions.
- Google Summer of Code is a verified alternative to an unspecified “Google summer camp.” The old CSSI landing page did not establish an active camp. GSoC is shown as preparation for a future cycle, not open 2026 applications.
- Diamond Challenge requires current high-school enrollment. Breakthrough’s detailed rules use age dates rather than a blanket school-enrollment condition, so graduates are not automatically excluded from that record.
- YoungArts uses grades OR age; the limited profile cannot fully automate that rule. Its official eligibility questionnaire remains the next step.
- Outreachy’s December 2026 initial-application window has closed. Girls Who Code Pathways, Foyle, and John Locke are also labeled for later preparation; no unannounced future deadlines were invented.
- CERN’s general pages differ about gap-year participation. That ambiguity is disclosed instead of assigning eligibility automatically.
- Cost is marked “Check program/event/project” or “Varies” where fees, travel, equipment, waivers, or host conditions were not established. A paid internship is not labeled as a free online activity.
- Citizen science, transcription, tutoring, open-source practice, and independent work provide accessible alternatives to selective seasonal programs. Participation alone is not described as publication, employment, formal credit, or an admissions advantage.

## Maintenance

Review source pages before each application season. Update the review date only after checking the record. Preserve stable IDs so saved checklists remain attached to the same activity. If steps change materially, migrate completion indexes rather than silently reinterpreting old progress.

Known application deadlines switch to “Applications closed” after their date. Exact organizer time zones and cutoff times are displayed in the record and must be checked on the final day. Age checks use current profile age; age-at-deadline exceptions, citizenship, residency, grade-specific alternatives, and other detailed terms remain manual checks. Unknown visitors are not assigned an example age.
